About Me

"I am a second-year Information Technology: Programmer Analyst student at NBCC with a passion for front-end development and UI/UX design. My background in retail merchandising and hairstyling gave me a strong foundation in visual design, color theory, and palette creation—skills I now bring into building intuitive, aesthetically striking web applications. I love bridging technical code with human-centered design to create experiences that are both functional and beautiful. Looking ahead, my goal is to transition into Project Management, leveraging my prior management and business experience to lead cross-functional teams and drive successful technical projects. My unique background, paired with the hands-on technical skills I’ve gained at NBCC, positions me to bridge the gap between clients, designers, and developers effectively."
